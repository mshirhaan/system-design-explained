    The kit behind it

    Recipe

    • 2:1 isometric projection. A world point (X, Y, Z) lands on screen at x = X − Y, y = (X + Y)/2 − Z. Every top face is a diamond twice as wide as it is tall.
    • No sharp corners. Each polygon vertex becomes a quadratic curve, which gives the soft, clay-like blocks.
    • Two gradients per colour. A light top face, a deeper side face, and the right side shaded another 6%.
    • One outline, one highlight. A 1.5px warm near-black stroke round the silhouette and a white line just under the front edge.
    • Warm blurred shadow. A brown, not grey, Gaussian-blurred copy of the silhouette sits a few pixels below.
    • Hard-shadow UI. Buttons and pills get a solid ink border and an unblurred offset shadow. That's the neo-brutalist half of the look.
    • Paper ground. Warm off-white with a faint isometric grid that fades out towards the edges.
    • Monospace everywhere. JetBrains Mono for labels, UI and body text.
    • Motion with weight. Packets hop along arrows with ease-in-out; labels pop in with a small overshoot.
