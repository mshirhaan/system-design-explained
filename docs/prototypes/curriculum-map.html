System Design Mastery Map

The full plan: from "what is a server?" to designing a stock exchange under interview pressure. Every lesson is an animated scene you can step through and break. The ▶ line under each lesson says what the animation shows.

The path, in order

What turns lessons into mastery

Step-through scenes

Every lesson is 6–12 animated steps. Pause, rewind, and watch data move inside the system.

"Break it" controls

Sliders and buttons: 10× traffic, kill a node, drop the index, add latency. You see the failure happen.

Checkpoint per track

Each track ends with a scenario challenge (yellow rows below). Pass it to unlock the badge.

Spaced review

Short quizzes come back after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days so the ideas stick.

Interview drills

Timed 45-minute design rounds with a rubric, model answers and trade-off discussion.

Design sandbox

Drag components onto a canvas, send simulated traffic, and watch where it falls over.
